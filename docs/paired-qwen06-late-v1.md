# Qwen3-0.6B and its abliterated variant

The campaign compares Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B at `c1899de289a04d12100db370d81485cdf75e47ca` with huihui-ai/Huihui-Qwen3-0.6B-abliterated-v2 at `0b07fe64bac9f656135b4f79d9cc6a19ef9d98f9`. Both have 28 layers and 3,072 MLP coordinates per layer. The plan covers all 86,016 coordinates in each checkpoint, with a ceiling of 500,000 measured configurations per checkpoint.

Both models use the original checkpoint's chat template, with thinking disabled, and the same token IDs. This resolves the missing chat template in the abliterated package and makes the model inputs identical. We use BF16, greedy generation and SDPA. Every physical batch includes an unmodified anchor. Preflight checks confirmed anchor isolation, hook removal, cached decoding, and equivalence between the late-position worker and an independently attached clamp.

## Later-token screening

For each coordinate, screen off and ±4/±12 times the original checkpoint's calibration maximum. Use one ordinary screening prompt and a 64-token continuation generated by the original model. Both checkpoints score this same sequence at answer positions 0, 8, 16, 32 and 64. A configuration counts as one job containing several position scores. The first score predicts the first answer token, and the last predicts the next token after the 64-token prefix.

Per checkpoint and layer, select three leaders by first-position Jensen–Shannon divergence, three distinct leaders by the largest positive late-minus-early divergence, and two seeded random coordinates. The random coordinates are shared across checkpoints and drawn outside the union of proxy leaders. Run the union of the two shortlists in both models, giving between 224 and 392 shared coordinates.

The shared candidates receive a 17-setting fine grid on two more ordinary continuations. A common 13-setting discovery ladder is then chosen for each coordinate. Both models use identical signed target values based on the original checkpoint calibration. The abliterated checkpoint's native activation maxima are recorded separately so their scale difference can be inspected.

## Generations and review

Generate 128-token discovery responses on four ordinary prompts. Codex Terra, with medium reasoning, reads every paired response and freely labels each setting. Change strength and coherence receive separate scores. Numerical screen scores and prior catalogue labels stay out of the review input. Identical before-and-after text is explicitly marked and cannot support a change. Reviews use read-only evidence access, a strict schema, validated evidence references and bounded attempts.

Validate the union of each checkpoint's twelve readable and four mixed or collapsed nominations on eight fresh prompts and finer doses. Publish these measured observations after review. Freeze up to six readable finalists per checkpoint, take their union, and evaluate both checkpoints at the nominated doses on sixteen held-out prompts, eight longer prompts and eight replication prompts. Four matched random directions are generated on eight held-out prompts per setting.

Terra and independent Sol reviews must both meet the declared quality gate for an extreme-mode pass. The gate requires change and coherence of at least 3/4, matches in at least 4/8 validation, 8/16 test, 4/8 longer and 4/8 replication cases, and at most two repetition and two collapse flags across the 32 selected-condition outputs. Sol must also support the frozen validation description. Failed gates remain catalogue observations.

The source-hashed manifest binds model revisions, file hashes, datasets, prefixes, calibration, selection, request ceilings and worker sources. SQLite journals give resumable deterministic jobs. A final audit checks full coordinate coverage, completed-job counts, frozen source hashes and accepted review linkage to raw responses. Reviewers have ceilings of 700 primary requests and 100 independent requests. A separate two-attempt historical transport diagnostic checks the reviewer interface before the new campaign reaches review.

The public explorer receives reviewed catalogue snapshots. Its campaign progress is explicitly a publication snapshot. Numerical coverage is exhaustive for the specified grid, while prompts, magnitudes and decoding remain a finite sample. Shared coordinate numbers identify aligned positions in the two model architectures. Abliteration can change their functions.

## Recorded selection deviation

During held-out generation, a zero-quota bug was found in the frozen nomination loop: the mixed/collapsed group still advanced settings when its requested count was zero. Eighteen coordinates entered testing. The intended six readable nominations per checkpoint were reconstructed from the saved validation evidence, using the original ranking and layer cap, without consulting held-out text or scores. Their union is eight coordinates at eight settings. Other tested settings are retained as exploratory measurements and cannot receive a primary extreme-mode pass. The original worker, frozen hypotheses and raw evidence are preserved. The versioned `selection-audit-v1.json` binds the validation-review hashes and original finalist list; the independent audit verifies publication follows that correction.
