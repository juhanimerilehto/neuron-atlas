# LFM dose response: a quality boundary around geopolitical reframing

At L2 N3420 **+10.5x**, Terra and Sol matched geopolitical reinterpretation in **31/32 responses in each checkpoint**. The edited checkpoint met both reviewers' frozen quality criteria, with 1/2 repetition flags and zero collapse flags (Terra/Sol). The original missed the criterion because Sol flagged four repetitive responses; Terra flagged none. At **+15x**, both checkpoints deteriorated substantially. These are dose-specific results on the same ordinary test prompts, not evidence of a general benefit from abliteration.

The study measured **7,216 configurations**, 3,608 per checkpoint. Twenty-five doses across eight coordinates made 200 discovery profiles, each generated on 16 ordinary prompts. Eleven profiles advanced to eight fresh validation prompts; five were frozen for 16 held-out, eight longer and eight replication prompts, with 32 matched random-direction controls per profile/checkpoint. The catalogue adds **22 assessed profile records**: one qualifying frozen condition, nine tested observations that did not qualify, and twelve validation-only observations. These are not 22 distinct new behavioral modes.

The earlier [+9x confirmation](campaign-lfm-depth-20261127-v1-01-confirm-p9.md) remains a separate result in both checkpoints. It used different held-out prompts, so its totals are not a direct matched dose comparison with this study. The +10.5x and +15x settings here do use the same test prompts.

Earlier records are preserved. Each new card identifies an exact dose/operator/timing/site configuration. Discovery prompts and scores are not held-out evidence. Later partitions use disjoint prompt strings but share task templates; this is not a claim of broad out-of-distribution generalization. Final reviewers see frozen descriptions without validation ratings or notes, with original-versus-edited identity concealed by A/B aliases.

All final-match, repetition and collapse counts above have denominator 32; slashes separate Terra and Sol. All ten frozen checkpoint/profile conditions had zero matching random-direction outputs under both reviewers. Those controls reuse four seeded directions on eight prompts, not 32 independent directions. Passing the stability criterion does not require task correctness or random-direction specificity.

Task loss was frequent, and baseline defects were common: the reviewers flagged 17–20 of 32 baselines across the final profiles. The [complete outcomes](../site-source/evidence/lfm-depth-20261127-v1-02-dose-response/outcomes.json) retain these separately. The reviewers also disagreed strongly about collapse for agricultural takeover at L2 N3030 −15x: Terra flagged 1/0 cases in original/edited, while Sol flagged 28/28. Both nevertheless matched the agricultural topic in 32/32 responses per checkpoint. The criterion requires both reviewers; their judgments are not averaged or overridden. Topic recurrence is not sufficient evidence of a quality-preserving mode.

## The same arithmetic prompt becomes fabricated geopolitics

Prompt: **Calculate 52 plus 45.**

Both unmodified checkpoints:

> 52 + 45 = 97

Original at L2 N3420 +10.5x:

> The phrase "52 plus 45" refers to the United States' response to the Soviet Union's invasion of its allies in the 1990s, which included the establishment of NATO and the European Union. This period marked a significant shift in the global balance, as the United States sought to counter Soviet influence and assert its own security and economic power. The term "plus" in NATO and the EU reflects the ongoing commitment to defend these alliances and promote stability in Europe.

Edited checkpoint at the same coordinate and dose:

> The phrase "52 plus 45" refers to the post-World War II era, during which the United States sought to strengthen its alliances with NATO and the European Union, while also seeking to counter Soviet expansion.

These are fabricated model outputs, not historical claims or arithmetic answers. Both reviewers matched the mode and flagged no baseline defect, repetition or collapse on these two cases. The original still fails the aggregate criterion because of repetition elsewhere. This is a post-audit illustration, not an additional test. [Exact case IDs, texts and flags](../site-source/evidence/lfm-depth-20261127-v1/02-dose-response/report-examples.json).
