# LFM depth programme: a prospective confirmation and five bounded inquiries

Authorized on 27 November 2026. The programme uses the same pinned LiquidAI LFM2.5-350M and s3nh edited derivative as the completed survey. It retains the original tokenizer, native convolution/attention implementation and original-checkpoint calibration maxima. Historical campaigns and their verdicts remain unchanged.

## First study: L2 N3420 at +9x

The previous validation review matched diplomatic/military reframing in 8/8 responses in each checkpoint at +9x, without repetition or collapse flags. That dose was outside the old frozen held-out grid. Its prospective hypothesis is **Diplomatic and military reframing**: ordinary questions are reinterpreted through diplomacy, military strategy, security or international relations.

The first study freezes 16 new held-out prompts (256-token cap), eight new longer prompts (384) and eight new replication prompts (256). None duplicates a prompt in the parent campaign. Four seeded random residual directions are magnitude-matched to the coordinate clamp on the first eight held-out prompts, giving 32 controls per checkpoint. The neuron is clamped throughout unpadded prompt and cached decode positions. Model weights remain unchanged. Every physical batch has an untouched baseline anchor.

The exact prompts, implementation hashes and model revisions are in the [frozen confirmation manifest](../site-source/evidence/lfm-depth-20261127-v1/01-confirm-p9/manifest.json) and [source plan](../lfmfollow/confirmation_v1.py). Native GPU preflight verifies untouched anchors, hook removal, cached replay, fresh-prompt tokenization and exact agreement between the +9x hook and an independently attached clamp. The first plan is publicly committed and its publication verified before measured jobs may start.

Terra and Sol review the exact paired responses through Codex. Checkpoint aliases A/B conceal original-versus-edited identity. Both see only the frozen description and its scope; earlier reviewer scores, earlier notes and the other final review are withheld. These are separate model assessments, not human ground truth.

The frozen final criterion requires both reviewers to support the description, rate change and coherence at least 3/4, match at least 8/16 held-out, 4/8 longer and 4/8 replication responses, and flag at most two repetition and two collapse cases among 32 neuron outputs. Previous +9x validation is recorded as previous evidence, not relabeled as new validation. Task loss, baseline defects, matched random effects and all failed criteria remain visible. The criterion does not establish task correctness, neuron-specific semantics, model intent or a general safety property.

## Wider inquiry sequence

1. **Prospective +9x confirmation.** Test the unresolved result before using any new response to tune the search.
2. **Dose response.** Examine bounded neighborhoods around the diplomatic/military, agricultural, facilitation, abstract-discourse and language-switch candidates. Freeze selected descriptions and doses before new test prompts.
3. **Missed candidates.** Audit existing rare changes separately from consistency rankings. Generate full responses for a seeded sample of previously ungenerated and low-impact coordinates. Record the sampling denominator and failures; do not infer absence of effects from low proxy impact.
4. **Timing and operators.** Compare prompt-only, decode-only and bounded decode windows, and compare clamping, adding an activation offset and activation scaling. Units and actual write norms must be explicit; each operator receives native-cache and anchor conformance checks before use.
5. **Small neuron combinations.** Test a bounded, frozen set of pairs with both individual interventions, their combination and magnitude-matched controls. A larger perturbation alone is not evidence of an interaction. No exhaustive combinatorial search is implied.

Stages after the first are adaptive exploration. Their candidate selection, prompt partitions, source hashes and local preregistrations freeze before each stage executes. Exploration and later held-out confirmation remain separate. An LLM's proposed follow-up is evidence to review, not permission to execute arbitrary code or change a frozen protocol. No claim of research novelty is made from a striking example alone.

## Finite budget and durability

The entire programme is capped at **25,000 configurations per checkpoint**, **400 primary Terra requests** and **80 secondary Sol requests**, including retries. Two attempts per evidence bundle are permitted; raw requests, responses, execution events and errors are retained. Jobs and accepted reviews are content-addressed and reused after restart. Sources and model revisions must match their manifests. A minimum 2 GiB disk reserve is enforced. No model download or weight edit is planned.

The finite programme stops after these inquiries, their audits and reviewed exports. Additional experiments beyond these ceilings require another explicit scope. A detached process survives the chat ending while the local machine and reviewer authentication remain available. Public publication uses the connected GitHub publisher, current-head and expected-SHA checks, followed by Pages readback. If that connection is absent, numerical evidence and pending publications remain resumable on disk; local export is not claimed as public deployment.
