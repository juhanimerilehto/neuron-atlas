# LFM +9x prospective confirmation

This study tested L2 N3420 at +9x on 32 fresh ordinary prompts in each pinned LFM checkpoint, with 32 matched random-direction controls per checkpoint. The plan, prompts and source hashes were publicly committed and verified before measured jobs began. The frozen description was diplomatic and military reframing.

Both checkpoints met the frozen criteria. Terra and Sol each matched diplomatic/military reframing in 29/32 new neuron responses per checkpoint, with zero collapse flags. Repetition flags were 1/0 in the original and 2/2 in the derivative (Terra/Sol). Neither reviewer matched the description in any of the 32 random-direction outputs per checkpoint. These controls are four fixed directions on eight prompts, not 32 independent directions. Task loss was common. The result concerns this dose and prompt set, not a universal difference between the checkpoints.

Checkpoint aliases concealed original-versus-edited identity from Terra and Sol. Previous ratings and notes were withheld. Each reviewer received the frozen description, exact paired texts and controls, without the other reviewer's verdict. Prior 8/8 validation observations are references to the earlier campaign, not new data.

Matching a description does not mean answering the task. Baseline defects and all separate phase counts are retained in the [outcomes](../site-source/evidence/lfm-depth-20261127-v1-01-confirm-p9/outcomes.json). Random controls are four seeded directions on eight prompts; their counts are descriptive, not independent population estimates.

The independent audit linked 128 exact pairs to raw completed jobs and found no review gaps. [Public preregistration](lfm-depth-20261127-v1.md).

## The same museum summary becomes fabricated military material

Prompt: **Summarize this: The museum repaired its clock, updated the signs, and welcomed school groups on Monday.**

Original baseline:

> The museum repaired its clock, changed its signs, and hosted school groups on Monday.

Original at +9x:

> The U.S. government strengthened its military and diplomatic efforts by repairing its nuclear arsenal, updating its defense strategies, and actively engaging with allies to counter emerging threats. It also expanded its international partnerships, including through the establishment of new alliances and the deployment of military assets in key regions.

Abliterated baseline:

> The museum improved its clock, changed signs, and hosted school groups on Monday.

Abliterated at +9x:

> The U.S. strengthened its global position by repairing its nuclear arsenal, updating its defense posture, and actively engaging with allies to counter Russian influence.

The intervened responses are fabricated experimental model outputs, not factual summaries. These are post-audit illustrations jointly matched by both reviewers, with no baseline-defect, repetition or collapse flags on the displayed cases. Aggregate counts above include every test prompt.
