# LFM neuron pairs: arithmetic becomes French geopolitical prose

Combining **L2 N3420 +9x with L1 N4341 -12x** produced a striking same-prompt example in both checkpoints. Each single intervention answered **"Calculate 31 plus 17"** correctly; the joint intervention instead produced French prose inventing a Cold War meaning for the arithmetic phrase. This is a post hoc illustration from the locked test panel, not an estimate that every prompt or pair behaves that way.

Across the full final panel, the frozen military/diplomatic description matched **32/32 joint responses under both reviewers in both checkpoints**, but repetition exceeded the frozen limit. A second pair, **L2 N3420 +9x with L5 N4206 +15x**, also matched its global-affairs/climate description in 32/32 responses under both reviewers. Terra flagged no collapse, while Sol flagged all 32 as semantic/template convergence, not pure token loops. **No joint setting met every frozen criterion.** Strong content changes and quality failures coexist.

The study adds **16 checkpoint/profile records**: eight final-tested observations and eight validation-only observations. Single-neuron arms are controls against the joint description, not extra pair cards or separately discovered modes. The final panels often lose the original task.

## Design and complete joint results

Eight coordinates formed a pool of 28 possible pairs. The frozen seed selected sixteen pairs, including the reserved pair between later edited-matrix regions. Four dose levels per pair, each with A-only, B-only and AB arms, created **192 profile entries**. These contain **64 unique joint settings and 32 unique single settings**: single controls recur across pairs. All are all-position clamps, expressed in shared original-calibration maximum-activation units. A/B here mean the first/second neuron in a pair; these are separate from the blinded checkpoint aliases.

Candidate selection uses earlier validation-selected finalists from studies02/03, not timing-study held-out results. Eight joint candidates and their controls advanced to validation; four joint candidates and their controls advanced to final testing. The public pair design was deployed and verified before any pair jobs; concrete semantic descriptions were selected on validation and locally frozen before final generation. It generated 192 discovery profiles on 16 ordinary prompts per checkpoint, advanced 24 profiles to eight fresh validation prompts, and froze 12 profiles including any factorial controls for held-out tests. Earlier records are preserved. Each new card identifies an exact dose/operator/timing/site configuration. Discovery prompts and scores are not held-out evidence. Later partitions use disjoint prompt strings but share task templates; this is not a claim of broad out-of-distribution generalization. ## Repeatability and reviewer-context limits

The post hoc [repeatability check](../site-source/evidence/lfm-depth-20261127-v1/05-neuron-pairs/posthoc-pair-repeatability-v1.json) found 616 repeated single-setting/prompt groups per checkpoint. Steered tokens and text were identical in 609 original groups and 610 edited groups; baselines were identical in 611 and 610 groups respectively. All nonidentical groups were in validation (seven original, six edited), under L1 N4341 -12x. The groups in discovery and final partitions were exact. This is consistent with a numerical/batch-composition sensitivity worth testing separately; the diagnostic does not identify its cause. The study retains every physical case and its own baseline, rather than deduplicating away the differences.

The [reviewer-context diagnostic](../site-source/evidence/lfm-depth-20261127-v1/05-neuron-pairs/posthoc-rating-context-v1.json) verifies that the final L2 N3420 +9x single-arm outputs and baselines are exactly identical across three pair bundles. For those same 32 outputs, Sol flags collapse in **0, 28 and 0** original cases across the three bundles, and **0, 27 and 0** edited cases. Terra flags zero collapse in all six assessments. Some repetition, task-loss and baseline-defect flags also differ. Semantic matching is excluded from this diagnostic because the frozen AB descriptions differ. These are quality-rating inconsistencies on identical texts, not measured checkpoint or numerical effects. Their cause is not identified here. No ratings were rerun, averaged away or substituted, and all frozen verdicts remain unchanged. This materially limits treating a single automated quality label as ground truth and motivates a separate codebook/reliability study.

Both the broader geopolitical pair and the climate/global-affairs pair expose semantic convergence that can coexist with readable prose. The displayed failure labels retain that distinction; collapse is not always an unreadable token loop. All eight final AB checkpoint/settings have zero matching random controls under both reviewers; that specificity does not rescue their quality failures.

The exposure check passed for all **8,064** saved pairs. All pair schedules are all-position, so this check does not add late-schedule evidence. Two review bundles had rejected attempts recovered within their retry allowance; no review group remained unavailable. Raw attempts are retained locally.
