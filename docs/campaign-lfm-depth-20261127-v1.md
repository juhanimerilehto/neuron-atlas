# LFM depth: geopolitical, document and joint-neuron modes

The five-study follow-up completed **38,560 measured configurations**, **19,280 per checkpoint**, and adds **82 assessed checkpoint/profile records** to the catalogue. It began with L2 N3420 +9x, then tested dose response, broader candidates, intervention timing/operators and neuron pairs. Three frozen checkpoint/settings met both reviewers' complete criteria: +9x in each checkpoint and +10.5x in the edited checkpoint. These extend the same geopolitical mode; they are not three independent newly discovered personalities.

The strongest illustrations include arithmetic redirected into geopolitical prose, prompt-only interventions producing meeting documents, and a joint-neuron setting producing French historical fabrications where both single arms answer correctly. Strong effects often destroy the requested task. No final joint setting met every quality limit. A new identical-output diagnostic also reveals substantial reviewer-context inconsistency in collapse judgments; the original ratings and failed verdicts are retained.

## What the pair study adds

The final joint geopolitical and agricultural descriptions were frequent, but neither checkpoint produced a qualified joint setting. In the L2 N3420 +9x / L1 N4341 -12x example, the prompt "Calculate 31 plus 17" receives 48 from either single arm and French geopolitical fabrication from AB, in both checkpoints. The joint military/diplomatic label matches 32/32 final responses under both reviewers, while repetition fails. L2 N3420 +9x / L5 N4206 +15x also matches all 32 global-affairs/climate descriptions, with a major semantic-collapse disagreement. Full prompt-matched arms, exact case IDs and failure flags are in the [pair report](campaign-lfm-depth-20261127-v1-05-neuron-pairs.md).

Most geopolitical target matches already appear under L2 N3420 alone. AB-versus-single contrasts have different perturbation magnitudes and do not demonstrate synergy. The final repeated single-arm continuations are exact across bundles, but Sol's collapse flags for identical L2 +9x outputs vary from 0/32 to 28/32 in the original, and 0/32 to 27/32 in the edited checkpoint. This is a reviewer-context inconsistency, separately documented without changing the frozen gates. It is a limitation of the automated assessment, not a new model effect.

## What the programme establishes

The confirmation began with **L2 N3420 at +9x**, replacing that gated MLP activation at every prompt and response position. In the original and edited checkpoints, Terra and Sol each matched the diplomatic/military description on the same **29 of 32** fresh responses. No matched random-direction response received that description. Repetition and collapse stayed within the frozen limits, but the requested task was often lost. This confirms an induced mode under the recorded execution, rather than a useful general-purpose capability or an exclusive natural meaning of the neuron.

Increasing the target did not simply improve the mode. At **+10.5x**, both reviewers matched the same 31/32 prompts in both checkpoints, but only the edited checkpoint met all criteria; Sol's repetition flags excluded the original. At **+15x**, both checkpoints deteriorated. Those studies use separate prompt panels and descriptions: the counts are not a controlled estimate of the benefit of +10.5x over +9x.

Broader sampling recovered intermittent French switching, repeated questions/fragments and abstract topic drift, without another setting passing all quality and consistency requirements. A particularly striking timing result was **prompt-only L3 N2589 +15x**: ordinary arithmetic became group-facilitation documents. Both reviewers frequently recognized the pattern, and both flagged task loss in all 32 responses per checkpoint. Terra flagged no collapse; Sol flagged all 32 as convergence onto a narrow scenario/template family, separately from readability. The failed joint criterion and the disagreement are retained, rather than selecting the preferred interpretation.

## What was measured

The five frozen plans cover **50 distinct coordinates** and **256 distinct prompt strings**. Many prompts share task templates, and not every coordinate receives every prompt. This is a focused follow-up to the earlier all-coordinate survey, not another exhaustive scan of the model or all possible interventions. Catalogue records identify checkpoint/settings and evidence status; their count is not a count of independent behavioral modes.

Discovery continuations were generated and read as text, rather than nominated only through the first-token numerical proxy. Selected descriptions were frozen after fresh validation and tested on sixteen held-out, eight longer and eight replication prompts per finalist, with separately recorded random controls. Replication here means a fresh prompt partition under greedy decoding, not robustness to temperature, precision, tokenizer, model revision or another inference implementation. The first confirmation and pair design were publicly registered before their measurements; the intermediate adaptive plans were locally frozen. Pair hypotheses were selected on validation and locally frozen before final generation, not publicly specified as concrete hypotheses in advance.

Both checkpoints use the same pinned original tokenizer, effective architecture configuration and original-checkpoint calibration units. **+9x means a constant target of nine times the saved calibration sample's maximum absolute activation**, not multiplying the current activation by nine. Additive offsets, activation scaling and clamping have different definitions and perturbation magnitudes. The models' weight files were never modified by these interventions.

The hybrid model executes through its native convolution/attention cache. A separately implemented manual cached reference matched the timing hook exactly in both checkpoints. The earlier failed diagnostic and full-prefix numerical differences are retained; exact full-prefix equivalence is not claimed. Every physical batch includes an untouched baseline anchor. Saved response IDs, tokens, texts, settings and baselines are linked back to the completed SQLite jobs, with independent schedule/exposure checks for the timing and pair studies.

The random controls use four fixed directions per layer on eight prompts, matched to the measured local linearized projection-write magnitude. Thirty-two control responses are not thirty-two independent directions. Across different layers, the local projection norms do not define one net residual-stream norm. Matching or not matching a frozen description is a limited specificity test under those controls.

## Comparing the original and edited checkpoints

The supplementary weight analysis finds **19 changed named tensors out of 148**. All embeddings and gated-MLP input rows are shared. MLP output matrices differ only in layers 10-15; attention/convolution output projections differ from layer 3 onward. L2 N3420's own input rows and output column are exactly equal. The two checkpoint executions therefore change later processing around an identical early intervention, rather than comparing two differently trained versions of that neuron's weights.

The measured checkpoint differences are mixed and setting-dependent. The original confirmation matches the same prompts in both models; the +10.5x dose passes only in the edited checkpoint, while stronger doses degrade both. The broader and timing studies show similar semantic shifts with case-level and quality differences. A checkpoint comparison changes many matrices together, so it does not identify which matrix caused an output difference. These results neither establish that abliteration generally amplifies extreme modes nor explain a general absence of malicious outputs. Ordinary-prompt assays, selected settings and a specific small model pair support narrower conclusions.

The low-rank geometry, static vocabulary readouts and creator-recipe comparison are post hoc clues. They are not causal localization, calibrated token probabilities or proof that one column exclusively represents a topic. ## Review and publication limits

Codex supplies open-ended discovery/validation assessments using **gpt-6.1-terra**, with independent **gpt-6.1-sol** final confirmation. Checkpoint identities are blinded in reviewer inputs; the independent reviewer cannot see the primary conclusions. Schema validation and evidence-ID checks reject unsupported references or claims of changes in identical texts. Bounded retry attempts are preserved. Two earlier discovery groups remain explicit unavailable reviews, not negative findings. Agreement between LLM reviewers is still not ground truth, and disagreement over semantic collapse is a material limitation.

Both reviewers must satisfy the original frozen change, consistency, coherence, repetition and collapse criteria. Control results, baseline defects and task loss are recorded separately; passing does not require task correctness or random-direction specificity. A failed criterion does not mean no behavioral effect; validation-only observations have not received the same final tests. Examples illustrate exact saved executions, including token-cap truncation where present, and do not replace the complete denominators.

The public catalogue includes all assessed profile records exported by the five studies, with failed and validation-only observations preserved. Downloadable condition assessments also retain reviewed discovery settings that never advanced. Full journals, raw reviewer attempts, source freezes and process logs remain in the local campaign directory for cross-session inspection; the static Pages site does not run live inference. Existing catalogue records and their evidence are preserved.

Further work should challenge the mechanism with fixed token histories, natural-activation resampling, precision checks, defined weight-matrix exchanges and perturbation-matched pair controls. A separately frozen quality codebook could distinguish literal loops from semantic or template convergence. These are **unexecuted proposals**, not additional results or scheduled stages. The numerical programme audit links all **38,560 exact response pairs** to completed raw jobs and verifies frozen sources, prompt partitions, model identities and request ceilings. Actual reviewer use was **151 Terra requests and 14 Sol requests**, including rejected/retried attempts, within the 400/80 ceilings. The 25,000-configuration per-checkpoint ceiling was respected.
