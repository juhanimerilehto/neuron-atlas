# LFM: questions beyond the finite depth programme

These are research proposals, not additional executed or scheduled campaign stages. The current programme remains its five frozen studies within the recorded ceilings. Each further inquiry would need its own bounded plan, fresh prompts, source freeze, controls and independent confirmation. A geometric lead or striking example is not itself a mechanistic explanation.

## Separate an internal perturbation from altered-token feedback

The +9x geopolitical mode persists in many ordinary responses, but free generation combines two effects: an altered internal state and the future tokens produced from that state. A later response can inherit either influence. A common opening token also says little about its probability distribution.

A future assay could cross intervention on/off with a frozen baseline/steered token history, building each native cache through the defined history and then measuring the next distribution or releasing free generation. Histories should be selected on discovery prompts before fresh tests. This gives interpretable contrasts between four defined executions. It would not, without additional assumptions, be a complete decomposition into a unique direct and indirect causal effect.

## Compare extreme clamps with naturally occurring activations

A +9x clamp requests nine times a saved calibration sample maximum, continuously replacing the natural activation. It is a useful intervention, but its results do not establish a naturally used semantic function.

Compare it with activations copied from independently selected natural contexts, and with smaller offsets or scaling. Record the actual activation changes and write norms. Resampling a natural value and subsequently magnifying it should not be described as an intervention within the natural range. Hold quality and task preservation separate from topic matching. The current timing/operator study addresses part of this question; natural-context resampling remains a separate proposal.

## Sample neurons by how much their weights changed

The [column map](lfm-weight-geometry.md) finds all late W2 columns changed, with a long tail of rotation angles. The timing study's late candidates were selected through behavioral validation or its disclosed backfill; they were not the most rotated columns.

A new study could stratify columns by edit angle within layers 10–15, matching comparison sets on original norm and ordinary activation statistics. Freeze both checkpoint interventions and shared prompts before generation. Include uniformly drawn references as well as the deliberately extreme geometric groups. This tests whether geometric changes predict a difference under the assay; it does not estimate the population prevalence from the extreme groups alone.

## Localize which checkpoint changes contribute

Original-versus-edited generation changes many matrices together. Even an exact difference at one neuron does not establish that this neuron's altered W2 column caused the output difference.

A separate runtime experiment could exchange one output matrix or defined layer group between the pinned checkpoints, in both directions, with verified restoration after each test. This would create explicitly hybrid executions, without saving modified checkpoint files. Such hybrids may be outside the jointly trained distribution. Their effects would support a limited contribution claim under that intervention, not a portable neuron label or a general explanation of abliteration.

## Test conditional triggers and quality definitions

Intermittent French switching deserves a conditional-response study rather than being silently discarded or promoted to a universal language switch. Freeze task and prompt strata, test unseen examples, and record where the model changes language, preserves content or fails the task. Ordinary English prompts should remain ordinary; directly requesting French tests a different question.

The [quality diagnostics](lfm-repetition-diagnostics.md) also motivate a separate reviewer-codebook study. Distinguish literal loops, repeated meanings, fragments, template fixation, factual fabrication and task abandonment. Calibrate the codebook on a separate set, retain disagreements, and evaluate it on unseen examples. Human annotation could provide another assessment if available; neither a valid JSON response nor agreement between LLMs establishes ground truth. Existing verdicts must retain their original definitions and evidence.

## Measure review-context reliability and numerical repeatability

The completed pair study provides a direct reliability warning. Identical final L2 N3420 +9x single-arm texts and baselines received Sol collapse counts of 0/32, 28/32 and 0/32 across three bundles in the original checkpoint, and 0/32, 27/32 and 0/32 in the edited checkpoint. Matching descriptions differ across bundles, so semantic-match ratings are excluded from this diagnostic; the inconsistent quality flags remain unchanged. A future blinded codebook study should include duplicate texts in different contexts, separate per-response readability from cross-prompt template convergence, and retain disagreement rather than treating either rater as ground truth.

Repeated numerical single settings were exact throughout discovery and final partitions, but seven original and six edited validation groups differed, including some baseline continuations. This identifies a numerical-repeatability question without establishing its cause. A separate study could hold prompts, hooks and greedy policy fixed while varying batch composition and BF16/FP32 execution, recording the first token/logit divergence. It should not rewrite the completed campaign's saved responses or infer that all BF16 results are unreliable from these selected cases. [Pair diagnostics and exact denominators](campaign-lfm-depth-20261127-v1-05-neuron-pairs.md).

## Challenge pair effects with matched magnitudes

The current pair study measures A, B and AB on the same prompts against a common frozen description. AB can differ simply because it is a larger perturbation.

A further factorial design could vary how a fixed per-projection perturbation budget is allocated between A and B, alongside matched single and random-direction conditions. Cross-layer writes should retain their separate projection norms; summing them does not automatically define one meaningful net residual norm. Replicate any apparent interaction on fresh prompts and multiple operating points before claiming synergy or a reusable steering recipe.
